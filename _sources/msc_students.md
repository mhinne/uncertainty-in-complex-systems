# Looking for thesis projects?

Are you interested in doing a Master thesis project with us? Go ahead and take a look at our projects and publications, and see if anything piques your interest. Contact me at max.hinne@donders.ru.nl, and we'll discuss the possibilities!

Some examples of potential projects include:

* Exploring context-free grammars of Bayesian model mutations.
* Extending the Wishart process to count data using copula distributions.
* Investigate the 'double descent' phenomenon in the context of Bayesian model comparison.
* Creating new priors for Bayesian model comparison.

Note: My supervision bandwidth is restricted so I prioritize supervising students of the Radboud University.
