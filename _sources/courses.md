# Courses

## Bachelor level
### Bayesian Statistics 

In the second year of the Radboud University AI bachelor program. This course is also highly interesting for PhD students in neuroscience or psychology that want to start with computational modelling. 

More information can be found in the [Radboud University study guide](https://ru.osiris-student.nl/#/onderwijscatalogus/extern/cursus/?collegejaar=huidig&taal=en&cursuscode=SOW-BKI203).

## Master level
### Bayesian Machine Learning

In the Radboud University AI master program we explore modern Bayesian methods for machine learning. We focus in particular on Gaussian processes and Bayesian neural networks.

More information can be found in the [Radboud University study guide](https://ru.osiris-student.nl/#/onderwijscatalogus/extern/cursus/?collegejaar=huidig&taal=en&cursuscode=SOW-MKI88).
